**Write**: Reflect on your previous work and how you would adjust to include ethics and inequity components. Total length should be a few paragraphs, no more than one page.

Ethics have always played a significant role in my professional life. My previous roles have primarily involved customer interactions where confidentiality and handling personal information were paramount. Whether managing data from the company's perspective or dealing with consumer information, maintaining ethical standards was crucial. However, when I ventured into digital marketing, the ethical landscape shifted dramatically. The focus was on leveraging data to maximize sales, often by any means necessary. This stark contrast made me uncomfortable, prompting me to reconsider how I could integrate ethical considerations more thoughtfully into my work.

In the realm of Machine Learning and AI, I encounter a similar ethical quandary. Modern companies often prioritize amassing vast amounts of data, sometimes overlooking ethical boundaries, to enhance their models. This practice raises profound ethical questions. Nevertheless, AI brings substantial benefits, such as user convenience and groundbreaking technological progress. Striking a balance between these advantages and ethical considerations is both challenging and imperative. As I move forward, I am committed to being more vigilant about the ethical ramifications of my work and to fostering a more ethically sound environment in my field.
